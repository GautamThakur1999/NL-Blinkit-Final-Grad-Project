# T5.4: Recruiting Plan

**Target:** Conduct ≥6 high-quality interviews. To account for no-shows, we will schedule 9 participants.

## 1. Sourcing Strategy
- **Internal Network (Warm Leads):** Reach out to university peers and young professionals in metro areas (Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Mumbai).
- **Screener Distribution:** Share a Google Form containing the `SCREENER.md` questions via WhatsApp groups and LinkedIn.
- **Incentive:** ₹500 Amazon or Swiggy voucher for 30 minutes of their time.

## 2. Participant Criteria (from Screener)
- Must be a high-frequency quick-commerce user (≥3 times/month).
- Must use Blinkit as their primary or secondary app.
- Must exhibit a "habit ceiling" (primarily buys groceries/snacks, rarely buys electronics, cosmetics, or pet supplies on Blinkit).

## 3. Timeline
- **Day 1:** Distribute screener.
- **Day 2:** Review responses and select top 9 candidates.
- **Day 3:** Send calendar invites and Google Meet links.
- **Day 4-5:** Conduct all interviews.
- **Day 6:** Synthesize findings.

## 4. Anonymity Protocol
- Participants will be coded sequentially (P1, P2, P3, etc.).
- No actual names, phone numbers, or email addresses will be logged in any artifact or commit.
- Verbal consent for recording (internal use only) will be collected at the start of the call.
