# T5.3: User Interview Guide

**Format:** 30-minute remote interview via Google Meet.
**Goal:** Validate AI-derived hypotheses around category discovery barriers without leading the user.
**Golden Rule:** Anchor in past behavior. Do not ask "Would you buy X on Blinkit?" Instead ask "When you last bought X, how did you decide where to buy it?"

---

## 1. Intro & Warm-up (5 mins)
- Introduce context: "We are speaking to frequent shoppers to understand how they buy everyday items."
- Reiterate anonymity and consent to record.
- **Probe:** "To start, can you tell me a little bit about your routine? When do you usually find yourself needing to order things online?"

## 2. Behavior-Anchored Walkthrough (10 mins)
*Ask the participant to open the Blinkit app on their phone.*
- **Task:** "Could you go to your order history and walk me through your last 3 or 4 orders? What were you doing when you realized you needed these items?"
- **Observation:** Notice the speed at which they reorder. Do they use the search bar, the "reorder" widget, or browse categories?
- **Probe (H1 Habit Loop):** "I notice most of these are groceries/snacks. Can you walk me through the exact steps you took to place this specific order?"
- **Probe (H4 Discovery Friction):** "When you open the app to buy milk, do you ever look at the banners on the home screen? What do you usually see there?"

## 3. Mapping to AI Insights (10 mins)

*The AI pipeline highlighted 3 major themes: low awareness, trust/quality anxiety, and missing information. We will probe these indirectly.*

**Targeting H2 (Low Awareness):**
- **Question:** "Last time you needed to buy a gift, a phone charger, or a beauty product urgently, where did you go?"
- **Follow-up:** "Did you consider using a 10-minute delivery app for that? Why or why not?"

**Targeting H3 (Trust & Quality Anxiety):**
- **Question:** "Have you ever considered buying electronics, cosmetics, or high-value items on an app like Blinkit?"
- **Follow-up:** "Can you tell me about a time you hesitated to buy something online because you weren't sure about the quality?"

**Targeting H5 (Missing Information):**
- **Question:** "Think of a time you were looking for a specific item (like a face wash or dog food) but ended up not buying it. What stopped you?"
- **Follow-up:** "What information do you usually look for on the product page before adding it to your cart?"

## 4. The Magic Wand (Wrap-up) (5 mins)
- "If you could wave a magic wand and change one thing about how you discover or buy non-grocery items online, what would it be?"
- "Is there anything else you think I should know about how you use quick-commerce apps?"
