# T5.2: User Research Screener

**Goal:** Filter for urban millennials/Gen Z who are active, repeat buyers on Blinkit but exhibit a strong habit-loop (low category exploration).

---

### Q1. In the past 30 days, approximately how many times have you ordered from a quick-commerce app (Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart)?
- [ ] 0 times *(Terminate)*
- [ ] 1-2 times *(Terminate - too infrequent)*
- [ ] 3-5 times *(Accept)*
- [ ] 6+ times *(Accept)*

### Q2. Which of the following apps do you use MOST often for 10-minute deliveries?
- [ ] Blinkit *(Accept)*
- [ ] Zepto *(Accept if Blinkit is 2nd)*
- [ ] Swiggy Instamart *(Accept if Blinkit is 2nd)*
- [ ] BigBasket Now *(Terminate)*

### Q3. Think about your last 5 orders on Blinkit. Which of these categories did you purchase? (Select all that apply)
- [ ] Fresh Vegetables & Fruits *(Accept)*
- [ ] Dairy & Bread *(Accept)*
- [ ] Chips, Snacks & Beverages *(Accept)*
- [ ] Beauty & Personal Care *(Monitor - if they select 3+ non-grocery categories, Terminate)*
- [ ] Electronics & Accessories *(Monitor)*
- [ ] Pet Care *(Monitor)*

*(We want users who predominantly select the first 3 options to validate the habit-loop hypothesis).*

### Q4. (Open-ended) If you needed to buy a phone charger or a face wash urgently right now, which app or store would you use, and why?
*(Looking for answers like "Amazon", "Nykaa", or "Local chemist". If they immediately say "Blinkit", they might already be an explorer. If they say "Amazon because of trust/variety", we have a perfect candidate to test our H3/H5 hypotheses).*
